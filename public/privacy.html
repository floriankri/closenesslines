Using the website

You do not need an account. The website does not use advertising trackers or analytics. Technical requests still involve information such as your IP address, browser information, the requested URL and time of access.

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Supabase operates the timeline API and database. These providers process technical data to deliver and secure the service. The legal basis for technical operation and abuse prevention is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in a working, secure website.

Drafts on your device

The creator saves your draft in your browser’s local storage, together with editing-link information when available. Hint progress and your timeline display preference are also stored locally. These values remain until replaced or you clear this website’s browser data. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to access them. This storage supports the requested editor and its settings (§ 25(2)(2) TDDDG).

Saving and publishing

When you save or publish, the timeline is sent to Supabase. It includes birth year and optional month, relationship types, any names or labels you enter, curve points, endings, life events, an optional tagline and creation time. Random links identify the saved timeline; the editing capability is stored as a hash on the server. Processing needed to provide your requested save and sharing functions is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

The named sharing link includes custom names and event labels. The public gallery response excludes these custom fields on the server, but includes relationship types, geometry, age, event types, creation time and your public tagline. Published timelines with at least two relationships can appear in the gallery. A timeline or tagline may still identify someone through context: this is not a guarantee of anonymity.

Anyone with the named link can read its names. Anyone with the editing link can change the timeline. Keep these links private. Please avoid entering sensitive information or other people’s identifying details without an appropriate basis.

Storage and deletion

Saved timelines currently have no automatic expiry and remain until deleted by the operator. To request access, correction or deletion, email us with the public or named timeline link so we can locate the record. Do not email your editing link. We may need to verify your authority before acting. Clearing browser data does not delete a server copy. Copies downloaded or retained by other people cannot be recalled through this website.

Providers and international processing

GitHub and Supabase may process technical data outside the European Economic Area. Their applicable contractual safeguards and processing information are described in the GitHub privacy statement, Supabase data processing addendum and Supabase data residency information. Provider-managed logs and backups follow the provider’s applicable retention arrangements.

Downloads and contact

Images and videos are generated in your browser; there is no separate media-upload service in this feature. If you email us, we process your email address and message to handle your request (Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR’s conditions, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability of your personal data. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact the address above. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, including the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA). We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.
