Relationships

The people you add appear here.

    How to draw

    Tap blank space to start a relationship. Drag its line to add a point. Drag a point to move it, or tap an inside point to remove it.

    Drag the end earlier or toward either outer edge to fade it. Choose Death to end it with a red ring. A line can move to either side; only its distance from you matters.

    With a point focused, use the arrow keys to move it or Delete to remove an inside point.

    Add a birth month for a more precise position of Now.

    Shared timelines with at least two relationships appear in the public Gallery without names. Relationship types stay visible. Anyone with the editing link can change this timeline.
