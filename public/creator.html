    Life events

    Moves, school, work and other turning points.

      How to draw

      Click blank graph space where a relationship began. On desktop, click beside the ages to add a life event. The Add buttons are an alternative, and work with a keyboard too. Drag its line to add a point. Drag a point to move it, or tap an inside point to remove it.

      Drag the end earlier or toward either outer edge to fade it. Choose Death to end it with a red ring. A line can move to either side; only its distance from you matters.

      With a point focused, use the arrow keys to move it or Delete to remove an inside point.

      Add a birth month for a more precise position of Now.

      Publish & share creates a named link and an anonymous Gallery link. Timelines with at least two people appear in the Gallery. Relationship types stay visible publicly; anyone with the editing link can change this timeline.
