How to read the lines

The straight black line is you, the person whose life we’re following. Scroll down to get older. The nearer a colored line comes to yours, the closer that relationship feels. Both sides mean the same thing. Lines fade as contact dwindles. A thin red circle marks a death. Choose a relationship to follow its line, or tap a line as you scroll.
