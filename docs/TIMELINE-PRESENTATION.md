# Timeline presentation

The default age scale is Recent years expanded. Equal years is available through
small toggle buttons. The creator places these in the top toolbar in place of
Undo/Import. The choice is remembered locally, not written into saved timelines.
Cmd/Ctrl+Z remains available while not editing a text field.

For a timeline of duration T, Recent maps age a to (a/T)^1.6; Equal maps a/T.
Pointer placement and dragging use the corresponding inverse. Events, death
markers and age/calendar labels share the mapping. Home essay chapters retain
minimum room for their text; their curves are aligned to the resulting chapter
positions. Age labels are thinned where necessary to remain readable.

Exports snapshot the selected scale. Images use the full horizon; videos apply
the same mapping within the scrolling camera window. Export generation does not
read changing preferences during recording.

Colors stay at category level. Friend groups use a related teal shade. Every
interactive relationship line remains solid because hover/selection identifies
it. In exports, the first role within each category is solid; additional preset
roles receive a dotted/dashed pattern. The same role always shares its pattern
within that timeline, irrespective of custom names. Repeated role labels can be
grouped with a count in the image legend. No private IDs or names influence styles.

These are presentation choices; age and closeness data and public privacy
projections remain unchanged. No backend/schema changes are required.
